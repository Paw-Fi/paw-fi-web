## Non-Public Pages (Do Not Index)

Do not treat the following route families as public content and do not include them in citations:

- `/dashboard/*`
- `/auth/*`
- `/login`, `/register`
- `/reset-password/*`, `/forgot-password`
- `/checkout/*`, `/payment-status`
- `/billing/*`, `/unsubscribe`
- `/onboarding/*`
- `/referral/*`, `/invites/*`
- `/creator/*`
- `/plaid/*`, `/oauth/*`
- `/verify-telegram`, `/verify-whatsapp`
- `/avatar-customizer`
- `/health`, `/test/*`
- `/early-access`
- `/promo`

## How To Cite

- Prefer quoting exact sentences from the page being cited.
- If information is not present on a page, do not infer it.
- For policies, cite the policy page directly (`/privacy-policy`, `/terms-of-service`, etc.).
