## Key Financial Concepts & Formulas

### Compound Interest Formula
A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt)
Where: A = final amount, P = principal, r = annual interest rate, n = compounding frequency, t = time in years

### Mortgage Payment Formula  
M = P[r(1+r)^n]/[(1+r)^n-1]
Where: M = monthly payment, P = loan amount, r = monthly interest rate, n = total number of payments

### 4% Withdrawal Rule
Annual retirement withdrawal = 4% of retirement portfolio value
Designed to provide 30+ years of inflation-adjusted income

### Emergency Fund Calculation
Emergency Fund = Monthly Expenses × 3 to 6 months
Higher for irregular income or single-income households

### Debt-to-Income Ratio
DTI = (Total Monthly Debt Payments / Gross Monthly Income) × 100
Keep below 36% for optimal financial health
