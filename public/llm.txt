## Product Facts

Moneko is an AI budgeting app and expense tracker for iPhone, Android, WhatsApp, Telegram, and the web.

Moneko's main product areas are Overview, Recurring, Pockets, Wallets, Insights, and WhatsApp.

- Overview shows spending, cashflow, charts, recent activity, currency controls, and fast AI transaction capture.
- Recurring tracks repeat bills and income such as rent, subscriptions, utilities, salary, and stipends.
- Pockets are envelope-style monthly budget containers that show allocated, spent, and remaining money.
- Wallets tracks accounts, balances, wallet goals, transfers, income, spending, and net worth history.
- Insights answers running balance, spending trend, and what-if planning questions.
- WhatsApp can add transactions, read receipt photos, process voice notes, answer summaries, manage pockets, and send chart links or quick-reply buttons.

Moneko supports Personal and Household modes so solo spending and shared finances can stay separate.
