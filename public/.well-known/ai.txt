## Product Summary

Moneko helps users capture spending quickly, organize monthly budgets into pockets, track wallets and net worth, manage recurring bills and income, ask AI-powered money questions, and use WhatsApp for budgeting actions.

Moneko supports Personal and Household modes so individual finances and shared finances can stay separate.

## Core Product Areas

| Area | Summary |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Spending, cashflow, charts, recent activity, currency controls, and quick AI transaction capture. |
| Recurring | Repeat bills and income such as rent, subscriptions, utilities, salary, and stipends. |
| Pockets | Envelope-style monthly budget containers showing allocated, spent, and remaining money. |
| Wallets | Accounts, balances, wallet goals, transfers, income, spending, bank connections where supported, and net worth history. |
| Insights | Running balance, spending trends, and what-if scenario planning. |
| WhatsApp | Transaction capture, receipt photo parsing, voice note processing, spending summaries, pocket management, chart links, and quick-reply buttons. |
