## Product Summary

Moneko helps users capture spending quickly, organize monthly budgets into pockets, track wallets and net worth, connect supported US and Canadian banks through Plaid when desired, manage recurring bills and income, forward receipt emails, ask AI-powered money questions, and use WhatsApp for budgeting actions.

Moneko supports Personal and Household modes so individual finances and shared finances can stay separate.
