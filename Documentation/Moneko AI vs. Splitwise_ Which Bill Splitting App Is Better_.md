# **Moneko AI vs. Splitwise: Which Bill Splitting App Is Better in 2026?**

If you've searched for a bill splitting app, you've almost certainly come across both Moneko and Splitwise. At first glance, they look similar. Both help you split expenses, calculate balances, and keep track of who paid for what. Once you start using them, though, the experience becomes very different.

Splitwise focuses on one simple question: who owes whom? It's designed to settle balances quickly, making it one of the most popular apps for vacations, group trips, and occasional shared expenses.

Moneko starts where Splitwise stops. Along with bill splitting, it helps you manage shared budgets, recurring household bills, monthly spending, AI-powered expense tracking, receipt scanning, and long-term financial planning. Instead of simply calculating balances, it helps you manage your shared finances day after day.

Neither app is the right choice for everyone. If you're planning a vacation with friends, Splitwise remains one of the easiest apps available. If you're splitting groceries, rent, utilities, and subscriptions every month, Moneko offers a more complete solution for everyday money management.

# **The Biggest Difference Between Moneko and Splitwise**

Both apps split expenses.

After that, they move in different directions.

Splitwise focuses on balance calculations. You add expenses, invite friends, and the app tells everyone how much they owe. That makes it one of the best apps for vacations, weekend trips, and occasional group expenses.

Moneko treats bill splitting as one part of everyday money management.

After recording an expense, you can also:

* build shared budgets  
* track recurring bills  
* scan receipts  
* review monthly spending  
* organize expenses with AI  
* manage multiple households or groups

That difference becomes more valuable the longer you share finances with someone.

---

# **Expense Tracking**

Both apps record shared expenses.

The experience feels very different.

### **Splitwise**

Adding expenses is quick and simple.

The app focuses on who paid, how much, and how the expense should be divided.

That's perfect for restaurants, vacations, and one-time purchases.

Once the trip ends, most people stop opening the app.

### **Moneko**

Moneko assumes your shared expenses continue every month.

Instead of typing everything into categories manually, you record expenses naturally.

Examples:

* "Dinner $64 split with Alex"  
* "Internet bill"  
* "Groceries $118"

The app automatically categorizes expenses, updates shared balances, and organizes your monthly spending.

Less manual work means you're more likely to continue tracking expenses over time.

---

# **Budgeting**

This is where the two apps separate the most.

Splitwise is not designed to help you build a monthly budget.

It records expenses after you spend money.

If you also want to understand where your money goes every month, you'll likely need another budgeting app.

Moneko combines budgeting and bill splitting.

You can:

* create shared budgets  
* monitor spending by category  
* track recurring expenses  
* review monthly reports  
* plan future spending together

For couples and roommates, this removes the need to switch between multiple apps.

---

# **Recurring Bills**

Most shared expenses are not restaurant bills.

They're recurring.

Rent.

Internet.

Electricity.

Insurance.

Streaming subscriptions.

Splitwise requires you to enter those expenses every month.

Moneko supports recurring transactions, so those bills appear automatically.

For households sharing expenses every month, this saves time and reduces missed entries.

---

# **AI Automation**

Splitwise intentionally keeps things simple.

There are no AI features.

Moneko uses AI to reduce repetitive work.

You can:

* log expenses with natural language  
* scan receipts  
* categorize expenses automatically  
* generate spending summaries

The goal isn't to replace budgeting.

The goal is to reduce the amount of maintenance budgeting requires.

# **Frequently Asked Questions**

## **Is Moneko better than Splitwise?**

It depends on how you use it. Splitwise is one of the best apps for travel and occasional group expenses. Moneko is better suited for ongoing shared finances because it combines bill splitting, budgeting, recurring bills, and AI expense tracking.

## **Which app is better for couples?**

Moneko is a stronger choice for couples because it supports shared budgets, recurring household bills, spending insights, and AI-powered expense organization.

## **Is Splitwise still worth using?**

Yes. Splitwise remains an excellent choice for vacations and temporary groups. If you also want budgeting and long-term household finance features, a more comprehensive app like Moneko may be a better fit.

## **Final Verdict**

Splitwise remains one of the best bill splitting apps for travel, weekend trips, and occasional group expenses.

Moneko is built for people who share money every day. Instead of stopping at balance calculations, it helps you budget together, track recurring bills, understand spending habits, and keep household finances organized.

If your goal is simply to settle up after dinner, Splitwise does the job well.

If your goal is managing shared finances over months and years, Moneko offers a more complete experience.
