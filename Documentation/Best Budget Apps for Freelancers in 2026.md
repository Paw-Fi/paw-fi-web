# **2\. YNAB, Best for Planning Variable Income**

**Best for:** Freelancers who enjoy structured budgeting.

YNAB's zero-based budgeting system works surprisingly well for irregular income because every dollar receives a purpose as it arrives.

Many freelancers use YNAB to separate tax savings, emergency funds, and business expenses.

The learning curve is higher than most budgeting apps, but users who enjoy planning often appreciate the extra control.

# **Frequently Asked Questions**

## **What is the best budget app for freelancers?**

Moneko is one of the strongest options for freelancers because it combines AI expense tracking, receipt scanning, recurring expenses, and budgeting in one app. YNAB and Monarch Money are also excellent choices depending on your budgeting style.

## **How do freelancers budget with irregular income?**

Many freelancers budget based on their lowest expected monthly income, set aside money for taxes as payments arrive, and track recurring expenses carefully. A budgeting app helps you monitor spending even when income changes from month to month.

## **Should freelancers separate business and personal expenses?**

Yes. Keeping business and personal expenses separate makes budgeting easier and simplifies tax preparation. Many freelancers use dedicated categories or separate wallets to stay organized.

## **What is the best free budget app for freelancers?**

If you're looking for a free option, Moneko, Rocket Money, PocketGuard, and Google Sheets all provide useful budgeting tools. The best choice depends on whether you value automation, subscription tracking, or complete flexibility.
