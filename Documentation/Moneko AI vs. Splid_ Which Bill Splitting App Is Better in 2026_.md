# **Moneko AI vs. Splid: Which Bill Splitting App Is Better in 2026?**

If you're looking for an app to split expenses, you've likely come across both Moneko and Splid. Both help people keep track of shared expenses and make splitting bills much easier than calculating everything by hand. That's where the similarities begin.

Splid is built around simplicity. You create a group, add expenses, and the app calculates who owes what. There are no accounts to create, no budgets to manage, and almost no setup, making it a popular choice for vacations and occasional group outings.

Moneko is designed for people who share finances beyond a single trip. Along with bill splitting, it helps you build shared budgets, manage recurring bills, organize expenses with AI, and understand where your money goes each month. Instead of only settling balances, it helps you manage your finances together over the long term.

Neither app is the right choice for everyone. If you're planning a vacation with friends, Splid remains one of the simplest options available. If you're splitting rent, groceries, subscriptions, and household expenses every month, Moneko offers a more complete solution.
