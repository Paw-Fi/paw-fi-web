# **How to use Pockets to organize your spending**

You can use Pockets in Moneko to automatically organize and track your spending.

Each Pocket represents a category like groceries, rent, travel, or subscriptions.

Once set up, your expenses are grouped automatically and your budget updates in real time.

---

## **How to set up your Pockets**

### **Step 1\. Go to the Pockets tab**

Open Moneko  
Tap **Budget → Pockets**

This is where your spending categories live.

---

### **Step 2\. Set your monthly budget**

At the top, enter your **total monthly budget**

Example:  
$2,000

This is the total amount that will be distributed across your Pockets.

---

### **Step 3\. Complete onboarding setup**

When you first start, Moneko will guide you through a few questions.

These questions help understand:

* your spending habits  
* your priorities  
* your budgeting style

Based on your answers, Moneko will:

* generate a custom Pocket structure  
* assign budget amounts  
* organize categories for you

This gives you a working setup without needing to configure everything manually.

---

### **Step 4\. Adjust your Pockets**

You can update any Pocket at any time.

Tap a Pocket to:

* change the name  
* update the budget amount  
* customize color and icon

When you adjust your total budget or a Pocket amount:

All Pockets update automatically to stay balanced.

---

### **Step 5\. Log expenses**

Start logging your spending:

Lunch 18  
Uber 24  
Groceries 60

Moneko will:

* assign categories automatically  
* place transactions into the correct Pocket  
* update your remaining budget instantly

---

### **Step 6\. Track your spending**

Tap any Pocket to view:

* total budget  
* amount spent  
* remaining balance  
* recent transactions

Use this view to understand where your money is going.

---

### **Step 7\. Reuse your budget each month**

At the start of a new month:

* copy your previous budget  
* keep the same Pocket structure  
* make adjustments if needed

This keeps your system consistent without starting over.

---

## **Customize your Pockets**

You can always:

* rename Pockets  
* change budget amounts  
* customize colors and icons  
* select multiple categories for each Pocket

Your setup stays flexible and adapts to how you manage your money.

---

## **Good to know**

* Pockets group your spending automatically  
* You do not need to categorize everything perfectly  
* Budgets update in real time  
* The system adapts to your usage over time

\# 7\. FAQ Section

\#\# What are Pockets in Moneko?

Pockets are spending categories in Moneko. They help you organize and track spending for areas like groceries, rent, travel, or subscriptions.

\#\# Where do I find Pockets in Moneko?

Open \*\*Moneko\*\*, tap \*\*Budget\*\*, then tap \*\*Pockets\*\*.

\#\# How do I set my monthly budget?

Go to \*\*Budget → Pockets\*\*, then enter your total monthly budget at the top.

\#\# What happens during Pocket onboarding?

Moneko asks a few questions about your spending habits, priorities, and budgeting style. Based on your answers, it generates a custom Pocket structure, assigns budget amounts, and organizes categories for you.

\#\# Can I edit my Pockets?

Yes. Tap a Pocket to change its name, budget amount, color, or icon.

\#\# What happens when I change my total budget?

When you adjust your total budget or a Pocket amount, all Pockets update automatically to stay balanced.

\#\# Does Moneko categorize expenses automatically?

Yes. When you log expenses, Moneko assigns categories automatically and places transactions into the correct Pocket.

\#\# Can I select multiple categories for one Pocket?

Yes. You can select multiple categories for each Pocket.

\#\# What can I see inside a Pocket?

You can see the total budget, amount spent, remaining balance, and recent transactions.

\#\# Can I reuse my budget next month?

Yes. At the start of a new month, you can copy your previous budget, keep the same Pocket structure, and adjust it if needed.
