## **Recurring Expenses & Income**

Recurring lets you automate expenses and income that happen regularly.

You don’t need to remember or re-enter the same transaction every time.

---

## **What you can set as recurring**

You can use recurring for:

* Rent  
* Utilities  
* Subscriptions  
* Gym memberships  
* Loan or debt payments  
* Salary or freelance income  
* Transfers or allowances

Anything that repeats can be automated.

---

## **Set up a recurring item**

Go to the **Recurring** tab  
Tap the **\+** button  
Choose **Expense** or **Income**  
Enter the details  
Set frequency and start date  
Turn on reminder if needed  
Tap **Save**

---

## **How it works**

When a recurring item is active:

* Moneko logs it automatically on schedule  
* Your budgets are updated  
* It appears in summaries and projections

You don’t need to log it again.

---

## **Why use recurring**

Recurring helps you stay on top of fixed spending.

Your regular expenses are automatically included in your monthly budget, so you can:

* See how much is already committed  
* Avoid missing payments  
* Understand how much you can still spend

It also makes your cash flow more predictable, especially when income is added.
