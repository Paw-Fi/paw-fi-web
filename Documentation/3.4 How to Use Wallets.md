## How to Use Wallets

Wallets let you track your actual money, balances, and net worth.

Before, Moneko focused on spending.  
Now you can also see how much money you have.

---

## **How everything connects**

Moneko has three layers:

* **Spaces** → different parts of your life  
* **Wallets** → where your money lives  
* **Pockets** → what you spend on

### **Spaces**

A Space can be:

* Personal  
* Couple  
* Trip  
* Group

Each Space is independent.

### **Wallets**

Wallets live inside a Space.

Each Space can have multiple wallets.

For example:

* Cash  
* Bank account  
* Savings  
* Travel fund

If you share a Space, you also share the wallets inside it.

Everyone sees the same balances and transactions.

### **Currency**

Each Space has one currency.

If you change the Space currency, all wallets update automatically.

---

## **Create a wallet**

Go to the **Wallets** tab  
Tap **\+ New Wallet**  
Enter a name  
Set an initial balance  
Choose icon and color  
(Optional) Set a goal  
Tap **Save**

---

## **What you can do with wallets**

### **See your total balance**

At the top of the Wallets tab, you can see:

* Total balance across all wallets  
* Monthly trend  
* Total income and spending

This gives you a full financial snapshot.

---

### **Use wallets when logging**

When you log a transaction:

Select a wallet

For example:

* Coffee → from Cash  
* Salary → into Bank

Each transaction updates:

* Your category  
* Your wallet balance

---

### **Set a default wallet**

Open a wallet  
Set it as **Primary**

It will be selected automatically when logging.

---

### **Transfer between wallets**

Go to the **Wallets** tab  
Tap **Transfer**  
Select **From** and **To**  
Enter amount  
(Optional) Add a note  
Tap **Save**

---

### **View wallet details**

Tap a wallet to see:

* Balance  
* Recent transactions  
* Insights

---

### **Track goals**

You can set a goal for a wallet.

Progress updates automatically as money is added.

---

## **How wallets work with budgets**

Wallets track where your money is.  
Pockets track what you spend on.

When you spend:

* Money comes from a wallet  
* It is categorized into a pocket

This keeps your balance and budget aligned.

\# How Wallets Work with Budgets

Wallets and Pockets work together, but they do different jobs.

\- \*\*Wallets\*\* track where your money is.

\- \*\*Pockets\*\* track what you spend on.

When you spend money:

1\. Money comes from a wallet.

2\. The transaction is categorized into a Pocket.

For example:

\- You buy coffee.

\- The money comes from your Cash wallet.

\- The spending is categorized into the correct Pocket.

This keeps your balance and budget aligned.

\# Troubleshooting Common Issues

\#\# I Cannot Find My Wallet

Make sure you are in the correct Space.

Wallets live inside Spaces, so a wallet created in one Space will not appear in another Space.

\#\# My Wallet Balance Looks Wrong

Check whether:

\- the transaction was logged to the correct wallet

\- the initial balance was entered correctly

\- transfers were saved correctly

\#\# I Do Not See the Wallet I Expected While Logging

Make sure the wallet exists inside the current Space.

If needed, go to the \*\*Wallets\*\* tab and create the wallet first.

\#\# My Shared Space Wallets Are Visible to Other People

This is expected.

If you share a Space, you also share the wallets inside it. Everyone sees the same balances and transactions.

\#\# My Wallet Currency Changed

Each Space has one currency.

If the Space currency changes, all wallets inside that Space update automatically.

\#\# I Want One Wallet Selected Automatically

Open the wallet and set it as \*\*Primary\*\*.

It will be selected automatically when logging.
