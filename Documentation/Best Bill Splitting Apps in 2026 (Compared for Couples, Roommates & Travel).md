# **What Is a Bill Splitting App?**

A bill splitting app helps multiple people track shared expenses and calculate how much each person owes.

Instead of manually dividing receipts or keeping track in a spreadsheet, you simply add an expense and let the app calculate everyone's share.

Depending on the app, you can split expenses:

* Equally  
* By percentage  
* By custom amounts  
* By income  
* By individual items

Modern bill splitting apps often include features like:

* Shared wallets  
* Household budgets  
* Recurring rent and utility bills  
* Receipt scanning  
* Spending reports  
* Multi-currency support  
* AI expense categorization

These tools help reduce misunderstandings and provide a clear record of shared spending.

**Best for:** Envelope budgeting with shared household planning

**Platforms:** iPhone, Android, Web

**Price:** Free plan with premium subscription

**Overall Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ (8.5/10)

## **Overview**

Unlike most apps in this comparison, Goodbudget is built around the envelope budgeting method rather than bill splitting.

Instead of asking who owes whom, it helps users decide how much money should be allocated to categories like groceries, transportation, dining, or entertainment before spending happens.

Couples often use Goodbudget to plan household spending together because both partners can access the same envelopes and monitor how much money remains in each category.

While shared planning is excellent, expense splitting is more limited than dedicated bill splitting apps. It doesn't automatically calculate balances between multiple people or simplify debts in the same way as Splitwise or Moneko.

If budgeting is your primary goal and bill splitting is only occasional, Goodbudget remains a solid option.

If everyone in your group already uses Venmo, it provides a convenient final step after expenses have been calculated elsewhere.

Kittysplit takes a different approach from most apps in this guide.
