## **Plan Your Spending with AI (Beta)**

AI Scenario Planning helps you decide what to do next with your money.

Instead of only showing past spending, it helps you answer questions about the future.

---

## **What you can ask**

You can ask questions like:

* Can I buy an iPhone before May 2026?  
* Can we afford a trip next year?  
* Should I upgrade my laptop this year?

---

## **How to use it**

Open the **AI Scenario Planning** feature  
Enter a question starting with:

**“Can I … before \[date\]?”**

For example:

Can I buy an iPhone before May 2026?

Submit your question to get an analysis.

---

## **How it works**

Moneko uses your actual data to evaluate the scenario, including:

* Current balance  
* Average monthly surplus  
* Recurring income and expenses  
* Budget allocations  
* Cash flow trends

---

## **What you’ll see**

For each scenario, Moneko provides:

* A clear verdict  
* The reasoning behind it  
* What it impacts  
* What needs to change if it’s not realistic

You get context, not just a yes or no.

---

## **When to use it**

Use AI Scenario Planning to explore different decisions:

* Compare timelines for a purchase  
* Decide between spending and saving  
* Plan shared goals in a Space  
* Test unexpected expenses

It works like a financial sandbox.
