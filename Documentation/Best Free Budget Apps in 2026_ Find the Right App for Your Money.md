# **2\. Goodbudget, Best for Envelope Budgeting**

Best for:  
People who enjoy planning every dollar before spending.

### **Why We Picked It**

Goodbudget follows the envelope budgeting method.

At the beginning of the month, you assign money to categories like groceries, transportation, and entertainment.

As you spend, each purchase comes out of its envelope.

Many people enjoy this approach because every spending decision starts with a plan.

The trade-off is manual work.

If you forget to enter expenses for several days, catching up becomes another task.

### **Pros**

* Excellent envelope budgeting system  
* Free plan available  
* Great educational approach

### **Things to Consider**

There are no AI features, receipt scanning, or automatic categorization.

### **Who It's For**

People who enjoy structured budgeting and don't mind manual expense tracking.
