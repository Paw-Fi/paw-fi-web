## Customer-question article checklist

This is a publication-ready backlog derived from the customer journey: discovery, onboarding, daily tracking, shared finances, automation, trust, billing, and long-term use.

**Use:** Publish one at a time

## How to use this document

- [ ] Work in priority order. Critical articles protect activation, trust, or data accuracy; High articles protect ongoing use and conversion.
- [ ] An article is ready only when every checkbox is complete, links are verified in the live product, and plan/platform wording is current.
- [ ] Write for a customer who does not know Moneko vocabulary. Use the customer questions as headings, search terms, and support macros.
- [ ] Where product behaviour differs by country, platform, entitlement, or connected provider, state the condition before the user begins setup.

## Priority order and article checklists

### 1. Start with Moneko: what it is and who it is for

**Priority:** Critical  
**Customer outcome:** Decide whether Moneko fits their personal or shared money workflow.

**Questions this article must answer**

- Is Moneko for budgeting, bill splitting, or tracking actual account balances?
- Can I use it alone, with a partner, for a household, or for a trip?
- Can I start without connecting a bank account?

**Publishing checklist**

- [ ] State the core job in plain language and give three suitable use cases.
- [ ] Name the mobile platforms and do not imply unsupported desktop parity.
- [ ] Link to the Free-versus-Plus comparison and privacy overview.

> Avoid calling it a bank replacement or investment/tax product.

### 2. Free, Plus, trial, lifetime, cancellation, and refund

**Priority:** Critical  
**Customer outcome:** Choose a plan without surprise feature loss or billing anxiety.

**Questions this article must answer**

- What can I do for free?
- Which features require Plus?
- What happens when a trial or subscription ends?
- How do annual, lifetime, restoration, cancellation, and refund terms work?

**Publishing checklist**

- [ ] Publish plan limits and current price/renewal terms with a last-checked date.
- [ ] State that bank sync is Plus and region-limited.
- [ ] Give exact iOS, Android, and direct-web management routes.
- [ ] Explain what remains accessible after expiry.

> Never make “lifetime” claims beyond the current published commitment.

- What exactly is deleted, and can it be recovered?
