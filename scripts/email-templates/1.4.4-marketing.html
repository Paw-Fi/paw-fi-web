We’ve just released Moneko 1.4.4 — a meaningful step toward making expense logging frictionless, wherever you are.

Our focus with this release was simple: reduce taps, remove context switching, and let you log expenses in the tools you already use.

✨ What’s new:

  • Telegram Integration with Moneko AI: Connect your Telegram account and log expenses directly in chat. Add transactions, check spending, and interact with your budget without opening the app.
  • Siri Shortcut Support: Say, “Hey Siri, log expense with Moneko” to record expenses instantly — fully hands-free.
  • Home FAB + Quick Log: Press and hold the floating + button to immediately log an expense using your preferred capture method.
  • Bug Fixes & Performance Improvements: Faster interactions, smoother transitions, and improved stability across the app.

We’re building Moneko to feel invisible — always available, never in your way.
